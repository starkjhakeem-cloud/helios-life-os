# HELIOS Navigation Pill

The bottom navigation pill is a locked HELIOS design-system component.

Locked behavior:

- Tab order: Home, Assistant, Goals, Tasks, Calendar, More.
- Assistant uses the HELIOS Energy Core.
- Home, Goals, Tasks, Calendar, and More use protected PNG assets from `assets/design/system/icons/`.
- Icons must stay locked to the approved nav slot size.
- Labels must stay baseline-aligned.
- The active indicator must remain centered above each tab.
- The pill must keep its approved spacing, radius, elevation, and bottom-safe-area behavior.

Do not adjust pill dimensions, icon size, icon offsets, label offsets, divider placement, or active indicator sizing unless the navigation pill is explicitly being redesigned.
